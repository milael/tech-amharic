Technology words in Amharic, reviewed by the people who use them.

An open termbase for AI, security, networking and software. Search in either language; spelling variants such as ሠ for ሰ still match.

This is a demo of work in progress. The Amharic terms below are first drafts waiting for native-speaker review, not recommendations yet.

Want an explanation instead of a word? Ask a question in Amharic or English.

    Ask a question

    Ask about any technology concept in Amharic or English. Answers are written in Amharic by the Gemma 4 language model, using the terms in this termbase, and list the entries they relied on.

    Answers are generated by AI and can be wrong; terms marked proposed are not reviewed yet. Questions are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to produce the answer and are not stored by this project. There is a small free daily limit.

    How a term gets published

    1. Propose. A contributor suggests an Amharic term for a concept and says where it is used.
    2. Vote. Other contributors vote. Votes order the review queue but never decide.
    3. Review. A reviewer who is not the proposer approves it as preferred or admitted, asks for changes, or rejects it with a reason.
    4. Release. Only approved terms reach the public dataset, the TBX export and the developer tools.
    Preferred
    The recommended term. One per concept.
    Admitted
    Correct and in use, often the loanword people already say.
    Proposed
    Waiting for review.
    Deprecated
    Seen in use but discouraged.

    Use the data

    Every release ships in formats people already work with. These files reflect the current demo snapshot, drafts included.

    • bundle.json for apps and the tam command-line tool
    • tech-amharic.tbx for Trados, memoQ and other translation tools (TBX, ISO 30042)
    • tech-amharic.csv for spreadsheets
